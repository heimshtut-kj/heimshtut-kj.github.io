Ad sizes

What is bleed?

Bleed = the background goes all the way to the edge of the page, so there is no white border.

Real example from the Heimshtut

Two real full-page ads from issue 0606 (Vayeira). Left: bleed, the color reaches every edge. Right: non-bleed, a white border stays around the ad.

  • Page size (page edge)10.5 × 13 in
  • Full page, bleed10.5 × 13 in
  • Full page, no bleed10 × 12 in
  • Text and logosInside the safe area

If your background or photo should reach the edge of the page, send a bleed ad at 10.5 × 13 in and keep text away from the edges. A non-bleed full page is 10 × 12 in and sits inside a white margin. Questions? Call 845-414-8007 or email ads@heimshtut.com.
